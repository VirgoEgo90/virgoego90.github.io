FAQ

Can I use the patterns commercially?

Yes. Patterns you generate here are yours to use in personal and commercial projects. A credit to Neon Sankofa is appreciated but not required.

Are these real kente or adire designs?

No. The motifs are original geometric studies inspired by the visual language of strip-weaving, resist-dye, and mudcloth mark-making. They do not reproduce specific named cloths or sacred symbols. If you want the real thing, buy from West African weavers and dyers directly.

Is my work uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is drawn in your browser. The share link just stores your settings in the address.

How do I get the same pattern again?

Copy the share link. It saves the motif, palette, density, and the random seed.
