    How to use a wedding countdown and timeline

    1. Enter the wedding date. The countdown shows the months, days, hours, and minutes left, and updates while this page is open.
    2. Read the checklist from the top. Items are grouped by how far out you are — twelve months, nine, six, and so on — and dated from your day.
    3. Skip anything that does not apply. A backyard dinner and a ballroom wedding will not need the same vendors.
    4. Print the page if you want a paper copy, or move the list into a binder. The printable 2027 wedding planner has calendar pages, a budget tracker, and a guest list if you want the rest in one place.
