Privacy policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026. This sample policy is a starting point; have it reviewed for your business and location.

What we collect

When you request an appointment we collect your name, mobile number, the style you chose, your preferred date, and any notes you write. We use this only to confirm your appointment and deposit.

How we store it

Requests are delivered to us by our form provider and stored in our booking records. We never sell or share your details with advertisers.

Your choices

To see, change or delete the details we hold about you, call or ask in the salon.
