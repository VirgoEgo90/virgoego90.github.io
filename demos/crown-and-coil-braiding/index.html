FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to arrive with clean hair?

Yes, please come with clean, dry, detangled hair unless you have booked a wash. We will confirm the exact prep when we accept your appointment.

How does the deposit work?

The sample policy on this demo is a 50% deposit to hold the chair, with 48 hours' notice for changes. Replace that wording with your own terms before you publish.

Can I bring my child?

Kids 12 and under have their own menu. Please say so on the form so we can plan the chair time honestly.

Do you provide hair?

This demo says clients may bring hair, or buy it at the salon when stock is available. Edit this to match how you actually work.

How do I pay the balance?
