Our story

A neighborhood hearth since 2016

Chef Marisol Vega opened Ember & Oak with one idea: cook everything over real fire, and buy from people we can call by name.

Our menu changes with the farms we work with. That means charred spring onions in April, tomatoes that taste like August, and slow-roasted squash when the evenings get cold. We bake our bread every morning and make our pasta by hand.

FAQ

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. We keep the bar and a few tables for walk-ins every night. Weekends fill up fast, so booking ahead is best.

Can you handle allergies and dietary needs?

Absolutely. Our full menu marks vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free dishes, and our team can walk you through any allergy.

Do you host private events?

Our back room seats up to 24 guests. Call us or mention it in your booking notes and we'll send our events menu.

Is there a dress code?

Come as you are. Date night and post-hike are both welcome.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount, at the host stand or by phone.
