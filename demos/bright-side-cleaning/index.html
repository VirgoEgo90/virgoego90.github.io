Checklist

What a standard visit usually includes

This is a starting list for the template, not a contract. Add, remove or split items so it matches your real work.

Kitchen

  • Wipe counters, taps and cabinet fronts
  • Clean the hob and the outside of the oven
  • Wash the sink and wipe the table
  • Sweep or vacuum, then mop the floor
  • Empty the bin if you have asked us to

Bathrooms

  • Clean the toilet, basin and shower or bath
  • Wipe mirrors, taps and tiles we can reach
  • Replace towels only if you leave fresh ones out
  • Sweep or vacuum, then mop the floor

Living spaces

  • Dust reachable surfaces and skirting
  • Vacuum floors and rugs
  • Mop hard floors
  • Make beds if the linen is already on them
  • Inside the oven, fridge or windows are extras on the quote

    Questions, answered

    Is the calculator the final price?

    No. It uses sample rates that you should replace with your own. The figure is a starting estimate and should be confirmed before a visit.

    Do I need to be at home?

    In this sample business, a first visit is usually done while someone is there. Recurring visits can use a key or lockbox if you agree that in writing. Replace this with your real policy.

    What should I do before you arrive?

    Please pick up loose floors, and put away anything you do not want moved. Pets should be secured if they might be unsettled by visitors.
