Last updated: January 1, 2026. This sample policy is a starting point; have it reviewed for your business and location.

What we collect

When you request a visit we collect your name, email, mobile number, area or postcode, the service you chose, any notes you write, and the estimated total shown by the calculator if you used it. We use this only to reply and arrange a visit.

How we store it

Requests are delivered to us by our form provider and stored in our booking records. We never sell or share your details with advertisers.

Your choices

To see, change or delete the details we hold about you, call or email the office.
