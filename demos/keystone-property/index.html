Overview

Light-filled living on a quiet cul-de-sac

Built in 2018 and lovingly kept, this modern farmhouse pairs an open, sunlit main floor with a private backyard under a 60-year-old oak.

The chef's kitchen anchors the home with a 10-foot quartz island, a walk-in pantry and a 36-inch gas range. Sliding doors open to a covered patio, so summer dinners move outside easily. Upstairs, the primary suite has vaulted ceilings, two walk-in closets and a spa-style bath with heated floors. Three more bedrooms, a flexible loft and a laundry room complete the second floor.

You're a 6-minute walk to Larkspur Park and Maple Grove Elementary, and 15 minutes from downtown.
