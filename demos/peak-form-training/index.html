FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need to be fit already?

No. Sessions are scaled to where you are now. The first visit is a conversation and a careful first workout, not a test.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes, clean trainers and water. If you have a previous program or a note from a clinician, you can bring that too.

Where do sessions happen?

Most sessions are at the Kingswell studio. Outdoor and online options can be arranged when you book.

How do I pay?

After your first session is confirmed, you can pay in the studio by card or bank transfer. Pack prices are listed as samples and should be replaced with your own terms.

Is this medical advice?

No. Peak Form is personal training and coaching, not a clinic. It does not diagnose, treat or manage any medical condition. If you have a health concern, speak with a qualified clinician first.
