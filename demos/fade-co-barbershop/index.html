FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, every day we're open. Booked clients go first, so weekday mornings are the quickest time to walk in.

How do I pay?

Card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or cash, in the shop after your service. Tips can go on the card.

What if I'm running late?

Call or text us. We hold your chair for 10 minutes, after which we may need to shorten the service or reschedule.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Absolutely. Kids 12 and under get a reduced price, and Lena is our go-to for first haircuts.

Can you do group bookings for weddings?

Yes. We can open early for the groom and his party. Message us with your date and group size.
