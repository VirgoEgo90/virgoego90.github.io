Privacy policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026. This sample policy is a starting point; have it reviewed for your business and location.

What we collect

When you request an appointment we collect your name, mobile number, the service you chose, and your preferred date and time. We use this only to confirm and remind you about your appointment.

How we store it

Booking requests are delivered to us by our form provider and stored in our booking records. We never sell or share your details with advertisers.

Text messages

We send at most two texts per booking: a confirmation and a reminder. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Your choices
