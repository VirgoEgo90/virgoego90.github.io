Questions

FAQ

What should I wear?

Garden formal. Think summer suits and dresses. The ceremony is on grass, so block heels or flats are a good idea.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We've included everyone invited by name on your invitation. If it says "and guest", we'd love to meet them!

Are kids welcome?

Yes! There's a kids' table, a kids' plate on the menu, and a quiet room if little ones need a break.

Is there parking?

Free parking is on site. Hotel shuttles run from 3:00 pm and back at 9:30 pm and 11:15 pm.

What if it rains?

The ceremony moves into the glass conservatory next to the barn. Same time, just as lovely.

What's there to do nearby?

Apple picking at Hollow Farm, the 3-mile Riverwalk trail, antique shops on Main Street, and Sunday brunch at Sugarbird Bakery.
