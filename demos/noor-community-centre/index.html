FAQ

Questions, answered

Are the prayer times on this page official?

No. They are sample clock times so the table has a layout. Always use the timetable your own centre announces, or a source your committee names.

Is this site a religious authority?

No. It is a website template for a community centre and prayer hall. It does not issue rulings, fatwas or guidance about worship.

Can I book the hall?

In this demo, room booking requests go through the visit form. Replace that process with the one you actually use.

How do donations work?

The donations button is a placeholder. Point it at your official page or processor. This template does not collect card details.
