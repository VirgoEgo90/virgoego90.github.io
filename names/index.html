    Naming tips that actually work

    Pair a time with a material

    "Ancestral Chrome" or "Future Clay" does the Afrofuturist move in two words: past and future in the same breath.

    Check before you commit

    Search the name, your country's trademark database, and social handles before printing labels. Generated names are starting points, not cleared trademarks.

    Let the brief steer the shoot

    Use the silhouette and accessory as a shot list, then read From Mood Board to Outfit to build it out.

    FAQ

    Are the names free to use?

    Yes, use them however you like. Because names are generated from common words, always run a trademark and handle search before using one for a brand.

    What makes a name Afrofuturist?

    Afrofuturism joins African and diaspora history with speculative futures. Names that pair heritage, earth, or memory words with cosmic, technological, or metallic words carry that idea.

    Do you store my saved names?

    Only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server.
