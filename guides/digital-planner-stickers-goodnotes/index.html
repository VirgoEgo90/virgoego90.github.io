Digital planning guide

How to use digital planner stickers in GoodNotes and other note apps

By Ivan Dummett · Updated November 18, 2026

Print a free habit tracker to decorateFree ADHD planner daily page to try in your app

Digital planner stickers are just images, usually PNG files with transparent backgrounds, that you place on a page in a note-taking app. They add labels, color, and structure to a digital planner without changing the PDF underneath.

Why PNG files?

A PNG can have a transparent background, so the sticker sits on your page with no white box around it. JPEG files can't do that, which is why sticker packs come as PNGs.

Adding a sticker to a page

Most note apps let you insert an image from your photos or files:

  1. Save the sticker PNGs to your tablet, in Files or Photos.
  2. Open your planner page and choose the app's image or insert photo tool.
  3. Pick the sticker, then drag the corners to resize it and move it into place.

This works in GoodNotes, Notability, Noteshelf, and most PDF annotation apps. Menu names vary a little between apps and versions.

Building a reusable sticker collection

Inserting from Photos gets slow once you have more than a few favorites. Two common ways to speed it up:

  • The app's sticker or elements library. GoodNotes has a built-in collection, called Elements in recent versions, where you can save images you use often. Place a sticker once, select it with the lasso, and add it to the collection. Other apps have similar features.
  • A sticker-book notebook. Import the PDF sticker sheets as their own notebook. When you need a sticker, lasso it, copy it, and paste it into your planner.

Tips for a tidy digital planner

  • Use stickers for structure first: day labels, month headers, and date dots make pages easier to scan.
  • Stick to a small palette. Three or four colors look calmer than every color at once.
  • Keep functional stickers small. Save the large decorative pieces for monthly covers.
  • Lock or flatten finished pages if your app supports it, so stickers don't move by accident.

Printing them as paper stickers

Most sticker packs include printable sheets as well. Print them at 100% scale on matte sticker paper, then cut around each shape by hand or with a cutting machine. Colors vary by printer, so print one test page first.

Where to start

Stickers go great with a planner layout you already like. The free ADHD planner daily page imports into GoodNotes as a PDF. Try it with a few stickers to see whether digital planning works for you.
