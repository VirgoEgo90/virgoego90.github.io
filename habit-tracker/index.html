How to use a monthly habit tracker

  1. Choose the month and year. The grid gets the right number of days, with a weekday letter above each date.
  2. Type the habits you actually want to tick, in short, specific lines: “Read 10 pages” is easier to mark than “Read more.”
  3. Print the sheet, or choose “Save as PDF” in the print dialog. Landscape often fits a full month more comfortably if your printer allows it.
  4. Keep the page where you will see it at the moment the habit happens — on a fridge, by the bed, or next to a desk.
