What this site is

Neon Sankofa is an editorial fashion blog for adult readers. It does not currently offer accounts, comments, newsletters, or a contact form that collects submissions.

Information we collect

We do not currently run our own analytics, advertising pixels, or account systems, and we do not ask you to create a login. If you later email us, we will receive whatever you choose to send (for example, your email address and the content of your message) and will use it only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

When this site is published, it is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages or a similar static host. The host may automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request times in server logs, under the host’s own privacy policy. We do not control those logs.

Fonts and cookies

Typefaces on this site are self-hosted. Visiting a page does not load fonts from Google or another third-party font service. This static site does not set first-party cookies of its own.

Affiliate links and Amazon

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. When live Amazon Associates links are added, clicking one will take you to Amazon. Amazon may set cookies and collect information under Amazon’s privacy policy. Until those links are added, the category placeholders on essay pages are not tracking links and do not send you to Amazon.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of the page will change. If you have a privacy question, use the address added to the Contact page once it is available.
