Policies

Privacy policy

Last updated November 18, 2026. This policy describes how Neon Sankofa and the ID Collectives site at virgoego90.github.io handle information. The site is operated by Ivan Dummett.

For privacy questions, see the contact page.

What this site is

Neon Sankofa is an editorial fashion blog for adult readers. It does not offer accounts, comments, or a contact form. Free printables are delivered through Payhip; if you choose to download one there, Payhip asks for an email address under its own policy.

Information we collect

We do not currently run our own analytics, advertising pixels, or account systems, and we do not ask you to create a login. If you later email us, we will receive whatever you choose to send (for example, your email address and the content of your message) and will use it only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

When this site is published, it is intended to be hosted on GitHub Pages or a similar static host. The host may automatically collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, and request times in server logs, under the host’s own privacy policy. We do not control those logs.

Fonts and cookies

The journal’s typefaces are self-hosted. Other ID Collectives pages (the shop homepage, free tools, guides, and freebies) load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details under Google’s privacy policy. The site does not set first-party cookies of its own. Some tools save your inputs in your own browser’s local storage so they are there next time; that data stays on your device.

Purchases and outbound links

This site does not currently use affiliate or advertising links. Links to ID Collectives products go to Payhip, which processes orders and downloads under Payhip’s privacy policy. If affiliate links are ever added, this section and the disclosure page will be updated first.

Children

This site is written for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated date at the top of the page will change. If you have a privacy question, see the contact page.
