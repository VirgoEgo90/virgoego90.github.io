About

A field guide with a memory

Neon Sankofa is a practical style desk for Afrofuturist fashion, streetwear, and design. The name joins two ideas: Sankofa, an Akan Adinkra concept often summarized as “go back and fetch it,” and neon — street light, electric color, clothes meant to be worn, not archived.

Plenty of writing already covers Afrofuturism as culture, film, or theory. What is harder to find is a consistent guide that answers a narrower question: what does this look like on a body, in a real closet, this week?

The journal decodes silhouette, color, textile, jewelry, and footwear; translates lookbooks into wearable formulas; and recommends gear only where it naturally serves the topic. It is written for adult readers. It is not a shopping dump, not a culture magazine in general, and not a storefront for any one label.
