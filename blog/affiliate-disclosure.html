Affiliate disclosure

Neon Sankofa does not currently take part in any affiliate program, and no link on this site earns a commission. Nothing here is sponsored.

The only products sold through this site are ID Collectives’ own digital packs, planners, and printables. Those links go to Payhip, the shop platform, and the money from those sales funds the journal and the free tools.

Some essays mention product categories — wardrobe basics, jewelry, footwear, garment-care tools — as starting points. They are not personal test claims or reviews of a specific listing.

If affiliate links are added in the future, each one will be clearly labeled, a short disclosure will appear on the page that contains it, and this page will be updated before the first link goes live.

Editorial independence

I do not accept payment from brands to insert a category, and I will not invent prices, reviews, or product tests. Retailer and product names are trademarks of their owners.
