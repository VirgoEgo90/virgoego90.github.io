    How to make a word search

    1. Give the puzzle a title and type the words you want hidden, one per line. Letters and spaces are kept; punctuation is dropped. A word has to be shorter than the grid.
    2. Pick a size. 10×10 is tight and suits a short list; 15×15 is a comfortable default; 18×18 and 20×20 leave more room for longer words.
    3. Choose a difficulty. Easy places words only left-to-right and top-to-bottom. Medium adds diagonals. Hard also places words backwards.
    4. Click Generate, then Print. In the print dialog you can choose “Save as PDF.” The answer key is on the same sheet unless you hide it first.
