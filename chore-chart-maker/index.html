How to make a printable chore chart

  1. Type a name or a household title, and optionally a “week of” date so you can print a fresh sheet each week.
  2. List the chores in short lines the child can read. You can switch the days to weekdays only, or leave all seven checked.
  3. Print the chart, or save it as a PDF from the print dialog, and put it on the fridge. Tick a box or add a small sticker when a job is done.
